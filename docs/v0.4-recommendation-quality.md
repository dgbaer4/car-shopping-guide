## Recommendation ordering

Confirmed and possible matches retain their existing eligibility criteria and are counted before shortlist ordering. The engine respects any known purchase condition, applies the maximum age to used and condition-unknown configurations, and skips used age and mileage filters for **New only**. Every configuration in the released EPA snapshot lacks listing-level condition. Both strict purchase-condition modes therefore require verification of actual condition.

The engine orders candidates by the number of unverified hard requirements, then uses evidenced condition preferences, body preferences and desired equipment. Within that level, it favors distinct model families and retains the existing representation of multiple selected powertrains. Estimated energy cost orders otherwise comparable candidates when the shopper selects **Operating costs**. For candidates tied on fit and applicable energy cost, the engine favors a model year that has appeared fewer times in that match category. Remaining ties use the configuration ID. The engine applies no year quotas and retains all eligible and possible candidates for pagination. [UAT-006 and issue #14](https://github.com/dgbaer4/car-shopping-guide/issues/14)

### Acquisition gaps

The audited EPA snapshot contains no additional entries within the five manufacturers and year window for the four supported powertrains. It also contains 25 hydrogen rows covering Toyota Mirai, Hyundai Nexo and Honda Clarity FCV, and two electricity/hydrogen Honda CR-V e:FCEV rows. The pilot has no supported fuel-cell classification or ownership-cost workflow. These models are absent from the catalog. Sparse examples include a single 2026 Honda Prelude, two 2026 Kia K4 Hatchback entries and a single 2025 Nissan Kicks Play configuration. EPA coverage alone cannot establish retail completeness by model year or grade.

Sourced pricing covers 112 of 1,253 retained configurations across 40 cohorts, including one of 37 PHEV configurations. Equipment links cover 27 EPA configurations. Body style, seating, listing condition, mileage and actual quotes remain unverified in the catalog. Safety ratings remain outside publication scope. Resolving retail coverage and acquiring more evidence are separate work.

## Purchase budget

**No budget restriction** skips fee inclusion and flexibility in forward and backward navigation and omits those answers in the v0.2 export. Normalization ignores stale values for those questions in saved profiles while retaining their existing behavior for other budget answers. The new answer applies no ceiling or budget penalty. **I'm not sure** retains its uncertainty, and **Over $30,000** retains its lower bound with an open upper end. Representative prices, manual overrides, tax, acquisition fees, annual registration and ownership-cost estimates are available in all budget modes. [Issue #10](https://github.com/dgbaer4/car-shopping-guide/issues/10)
